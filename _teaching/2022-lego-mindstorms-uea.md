Designed and delivered hands-on Lego Mindstorms workshops at the University of East Anglia for visiting school groups and prospective students. The sessions introduced participants to core ideas in robotics — sensing, actuation, and simple control loops — by having them build and program small autonomous robots to complete navigation and line-following challenges.

The workshops were run through UEA's School of Computing Sciences as part of broader outreach activity associated with the AgriFoRwArdS Centre for Doctoral Training. They emphasised that robotics research is iterative and accessible: a robot that "almost works" is normal, and debugging is part of the process. I scaffolded the sessions so that students with no prior programming experience could still leave having made their robot move, while more confident participants were given stretch challenges (obstacle avoidance, multi-sensor logic).

This was the original campus-based version of the outreach programme that later evolved into the off-site "Bringing Scientists to You" school visits described in [2023 Lego Mindstorms outreach](/teaching/2023-lego-mindstorms-outreach).
