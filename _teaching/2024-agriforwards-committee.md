Served on two organisation committees for the AgriFoRwArdS CDT during 2024:

- **Programme Committee, Annual Conference 2024** — Helped shape the technical programme for the CDT's flagship annual conference, including reviewing submissions, structuring sessions, and chairing presentation slots. The Annual Conference is the main venue where cohort members across all partner institutions (UEA, Lincoln, Cambridge) present progress to peers, supervisors, and industry partners.
- **Summer School 2024 co-organiser** — Co-organised the AgriFoRwArdS Summer School, a multi-day training event that brings together CDT students for technical sessions, industry talks, and team activities. Responsibilities included scheduling sessions, coordinating speakers, and managing logistics on the day.

Both roles were unpaid service to the CDT community and ran alongside my final-year PhD work. They complemented earlier teaching contributions — see also the [Sensor CDT Team Challenge mentoring](/teaching/2023-sensor-cdt-mentoring) and the public-engagement work for [Drink Outside the Box](/teaching/2024-drink-outside-the-box).
