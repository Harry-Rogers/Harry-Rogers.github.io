A summary of public-engagement, schools, and AgriFoRwArdS CDT activities run alongside research, mostly during 2020–2024 at UEA / Lincoln.

## Public engagement

- **LEGO Robot Tutorials** at UEA, Great Yarmouth College, and Thetford Academy.
- **AgriFoRwArdS Electric Egg** promotional video filming.
- **Public speaking at Ormiston Victory Academy** — careers session for secondary-school students aiming for STEM degrees.
- **Meet the Scientist panel** at Ormiston Victory Academy for the Norwich Science Festival.

- **AgriFoRwArdS Lincoln Spring School** — robotic chef competition.
