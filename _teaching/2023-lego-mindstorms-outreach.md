Delivered Lego Mindstorms robotics workshops at Yarmouth College and Thetford Academy as part of the University of East Anglia's *Bringing Scientists to You* programme — a widening-participation initiative that takes UEA researchers into schools and colleges whose students are less likely to visit a university campus on their own.

Each session walked groups of secondary-school and college-age students through building a small autonomous robot, then programming it to react to its environment using light, touch, and ultrasonic sensors. Beyond the technical content, the workshops aimed to make a postgraduate researcher feel like a real person rather than an abstraction: I talked about how I had got to a PhD from a computer-science background, what agricultural robotics actually looks like day-to-day, and why precision spraying matters for sustainable farming.

The off-site format meant adapting the campus version of the workshop (see [the UEA workshops](/teaching/2022-lego-mindstorms-uea)) to a single classroom, a fixed time slot, and equipment that had to be transported and set up on arrival.
