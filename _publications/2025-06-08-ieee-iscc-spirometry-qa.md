## Abstract

Home spirometry promises continuous respiratory monitoring outside the clinic but suffers from variable manoeuvre quality without trained supervision. We present a machine-learning quality-assurance system that flags poor-quality spirometry efforts in real time, enabling self-correction and producing clinic-grade data from unsupervised home recordings.
