## Abstract

We present an automated pipeline for scoring the Clock Drawing Test — a widely used cognitive-screening instrument — using deep learning. Saliency maps are used to surface which regions of the drawing drove the model's score, supporting clinician trust and downstream explainability of cognitive assessment.
