## Abstract

This paper evaluates how quantization affects the interpretability of convolutional neural networks deployed on resource-constrained hardware (e.g. embedded agricultural devices). We compare saliency-map agreement between full-precision and quantized models and assess the trade-off between deployability and explanation fidelity. Recognised with the **Best Paper Award** at KDIR 2023.
