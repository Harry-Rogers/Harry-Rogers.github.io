## Abstract

We introduce a set of domain-specific image augmentations tailored to precision spraying scenes (lighting variation, occlusion, motion blur, droplet artefacts) and use them to systematically robustness-test convolutional neural networks used for spray-deposit evaluation. Results show meaningful improvements in out-of-distribution robustness compared to generic augmentation pipelines.
